STUDY AND WORK: Your Pathway to Career Success

Empowering Job Seekers, Enhancing Workforce Productivity

STUDY AND WORK is a leading career management and recruitment agency dedicated to bridging the gap between skilled job seekers and Australian employers. We specialize in connecting trainees, graduates, and skilled migrants with businesses looking to enhance productivity in a cost-effective and efficient manner.

Since 2007, we have successfully placed over 8,000 job seekers into roles across 3,000+ reputable Australian companies. Many of our candidates, who started in entry-level or junior positions, have progressed to senior roles such as Financial Controllers, Accountants, Programmers, System Administrators, Business Analysts, HR Consultants, and Design Engineers, enjoying competitive salaries and fulfilling careers.

Building Careers & Changing Lives

We challenge employers' misconceptions by introducing them to candidates like you—those who may lack extensive work experience but are motivated, hardworking, and eager to learn and grow. Our innovative "Try Before Hire" staffing solutions minimize hiring risks, giving employers the confidence to invest in fresh talent.

We Work for You

We don’t just find you a job—we market you to potential employers by highlighting your strengths, expertise, academic achievements, multilingual abilities, cultural advantages, work rights, and professional experience (whether in Australia or overseas). Acting as your advocate, we ensure your skills and potential are recognized—because having someone else vouch for you is always more impactful!
