Frequently Asked Questions

How do you ensure the quality of the interns?
Our interns undergo a rigorous pre-screening process, which includes assessments of their communication, interpersonal and soft skills, in addition to their academic qualifications. We select only candidates from prestigious institutions.
How do you match interns with our specific business needs and industry?
Our expert candidate managers and talent acquisition specialists have a deep understanding of both Australian business requirements and the eagerness of candidates for knowledge. Our process starts with a thorough analysis of your business needs as a basis for sourcing the perfect candidates.
Are there any additional costs for the insurance or training plans?
No, all these are included in the program cost. We handle the insurance, training plans and performance reviews as part of our comprehensive service.
Are there any support services provided during the intern's placement?
Yes, we provide continuous support throughout the internship. This includes regular performance reviews and guidance on best practices for integrating the intern into your team. Our support is designed to ensure both the intern and your business gain the maximum benefit from the program.
Is it legal to host an unpaid intern?
Yes, an unpaid internship is lawful if it’s part of a vocational placement or if no employment relationship exists. This ensures compliance and enriching learning experiences. The Fair Work Ombudsman clarifies these opportunities as ideal for bridging the gap between study and professional life, often leading to future employment. They offer a win-win scenario: while interns gain real-world experience, your company benefits from fresh perspectives and potential future talent.
Can we choose to hire the intern after the program?
Absolutely! Over 70% of our interns become valuable long-term team members. We encourage such transitions.
