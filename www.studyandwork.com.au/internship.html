The Frustrating Paradox

Many students graduate with qualifications but struggle to find jobs. Unfortunately, many students face the frustrating paradox: “You need experience to get a job, but you need a job to get experience.”

Employers increasingly ask for:

  • Australian Work Experience
  • Industry Exposure
  • Professional References
  • Practical Skills

That's Where We Come In

At STUDY AND WORK, we bridge the gap between education and employment by helping students secure professional internships in their field of study.

This allows them to practice their skills to prove their worth to employers. We are a recruitment company that understands what employers are looking for and how to help candidates become job-ready.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the internship paid?

Most internships are unpaid as they are structured learning opportunities designed to help students gain experience and improve employability.

How long is the internship?

Typically 12 weeks, usually 3 days per week, depending on the employer.

Will I get a job after?

Employment is not guaranteed; however, many employers hire interns who perform well and become productive.

Can I do an internship while studying?

Yes. Many students complete internships alongside their studies.
