Program Options

Most internships start as unpaid positions because they are not jobs. However, if you have previous work experience and can contribute effectively from day one, the internship may be paid from the beginning. Please mention Yes/No next to each of the options.

Unpaid Internship Placement

We'll secure your internship position by promoting your profile to our network of host companies and advocate for 12 weeks of unpaid internship offer. As you become productive, we will also strive to persuade the company to offer you a job. Program Facilitation Fee for organising internship agreement, training plan, work experience insurance, performance reviews, and certificate of work experience is payable upon successful placement in an internship and we provide a Satisfaction Guarantee: All internships, except Engineering and IT: $2,000+GST | Engineering and IT internships: $2,500+GST

Paid Job Placement

We'll secure you a paid job by promoting your profile to potential employers and advocate for 1 year of paid job placement with full-time or part-time employment offer with an annual salary of $70K-$90K. Program Facilitation Fee is payable upon successful placement in a job, which will be guaranteed for 12 months: 10% of the annual salary package+GST

Program Deposit Requirement

Please note that a fully refundable $55 deposit is required before you attend the interview with a potential host company, if you are applying for a self-funded internship or job placement.

Study and Work offers a full refund of the deposit if we are unable to secure at least three interview opportunities or one internship placement within eight weeks of the application. If this minimum is met, the deposit becomes non-refundable.

Upon successful interview with the prospective host company and you accepting the internship offer, you will be required to pay the relevant internship facilitation fee minus the $55 deposit. Thank you.

Terms and Conditions of Professional Internship Program

The acceptance of internship offer will be taken as acceptance of the Terms and Conditions listed below:

Fees for Service - Professional Internship Program
STUDY AND WORK provides internship placement for interns who desire to undertake an internship with a nominated host company. These internship placements are used as "Work Experience" and the interns are required to pay the agreed program fee after receiving the confirmed placement offer and prior to starting the internship placement (nil fees apply if you are competing for placement).

Professional Internship Program is designed to provide work experience to assist in developing skills relevant to your field of study and will involve the observation of tasks performed by employees of the host company or the performance of tasks under close supervision of the host company. The internship will be unpaid if the placement benefits the intern more than the host company. The internship will be paid if the placement benefits the host company more than the intern. The internship will be for the duration of 12 weeks, and you can work between 3 to 5 days per week. The program fee is for our service that includes organising placement, internship agreement, training plan, insurance, and providing ongoing support during the period of placement.

Internship Placement and Satisfaction Guarantee
STUDY AND WORK guarantees meaningful and study relevant work experience at host company for the period of the internship. This means that if you are not completely satisfied with the work experience offered by host company or your internship gets terminated immaturely, then STUDY AND WORK will find a replacement internship at no extra charge.

Internship Placement Process
STUDY AND WORK will do everything possible to place you on your intended start date, however due to the differing nature of every company and their differing time and financial commitments, the exact intended start date may not be available. Upon successful application, STUDY AND WORK will arrange and prepare you for interviews with the prospective host companies. If you are selected, STUDY AND WORK will send you a Confirmation of Placement with an invoice to make necessary payment of program fees (nil fees apply if you are competing for placement). Once the program fees have been paid, STUDY AND WORK will request host company to complete your Internship Agreement and Training Plan and organise your work experience insurance so that you can commence your internship on the agreed date. STUDY AND WORK will try their best to convince the host company to hire you if you are productive during the internship.

Our Relationship
STUDY AND WORK is in no way responsible for any damages caused by the intern during the internship placement, regardless of the nature of the damages. The intern accepts full responsibility for their actions. STUDY AND WORK is a facilitator of Professional Internship Program and is not seen as the employer, contracting company or any other mechanism that may constitute the employer/employee relationship with the applicant.
