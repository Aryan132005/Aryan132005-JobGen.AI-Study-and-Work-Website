Professional Job Placement


If you think you have the necessary skills, qualification, and relevant work experience, we want to hear from you today! You deserve a better job.

Our main objective is to build a strong bridge between the experienced professionals and the employers who are looking for enthusiastic and qualified staff.

If you have worked hard to develop your skills, experience, and talent, we can partner with you in finding an employer who values your commitment and dedication.

We are proud to work with capable candidates like you to meet Australian companies' need for quality staff. We will work closely with you during the recruitment process and ensure that you are placed in the role you deserve.

We are a compassionate recruitment agency and we welcome applications from everyone who wants to and can work in Australia, including skilled migrants without local experience.

We have job opportunities in mainly the following areas:

Accounting
Administration
Human Resource Management
Marketing
Supply Chain Management
Information Technology
Engineering
Customer Service

Please register online to be considered for current and future suitable job opportunities. Nil fees apply.

About Us

STUDYANDWORK is a unique career management and recruitment agency that has successfully tapped into the skills of students, graduates and permanent skilled migrants to meet the staffing needs of Australian employers - cost-effectively and efficiently. We offer aspiring job seekers a rare opportunity to facilitate their transition from study or overseas profession to a successful career in Australia through our internship, graduate and professional job placement programs.
