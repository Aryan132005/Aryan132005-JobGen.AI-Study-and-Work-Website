Are You Looking To Hire Flexible Staff?

We can help. STUDY AND WORK is the first talent recruitment agency in Australia to focus entirely on students and graduates. As such, we have access to a talent pool of qualified and available candidates seeking flexible jobs for a better work-life-study balance.

Our candidates are flexible, hardworking, reliable, smart, skilled, readily available, and willing to work. They are open to casual and part-time job opportunities and are best suited for businesses’ fluctuating demand for workforce.

To register in our Talent Pool, as a minimum, all the candidates must have:

  • Previous experience in the relevant field
  • Correct Australian working rights
  • Excellent communication and customer service skills
  • Industry certifications
  • Availability on a short-notice
  • Skills to hit the ground running!


Let us meet your staffing needs and save you time, effort, and thousands of dollars in staffing costs.

How Does It Work?

We will source the best possible staff and you can hire them in your payroll as your employees for as long as you wish at the wages negotiated with them directly.


How Much Does It Cost?

Our services offer three distinct options to meet your needs:

Direct-Hire Staffing: Our comprehensive approach involves sourcing, screening, and presenting the most qualified candidates for your consideration. Upon successful placement, a recruitment fee of $900+GST, applies depending on the number of the roles to be filled. You have the flexibility to choose between casual, temporary, or permanent hires. Should the selected candidate prove unsuitable or depart within the initial three months, we offer a replacement service at no extra charge.

On-Hire Staffing: Opting for this solution means we handle the sourcing, screening, and employment of suitable candidates on a casual contract basis. Our transparent hourly charge rate covers the gross wage for staff, casual loading, superannuation, associated costs, and our service fee, billed weekly post-job completion. It starts at $42+GST per hour.

We kindly request your input on which option aligns best with your requirements and send us a brief job description for the vacant role, we will initiate the search process accordingly.
