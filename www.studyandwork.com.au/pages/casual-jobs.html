FARM HARVEST JOBS

From grape harvesting in Berri, to mango picking in Darwin, each year thousands of people find work helping to bring in the fruit and vegetable harvests. It offers a great way for people to travel around Australia at their own pace while working and earning money.
Workforce Australia links job seekers with harvest jobs Australia wide. Visit their website to find a harvest job, locate a harvest labour provider, and search town and crop information.
