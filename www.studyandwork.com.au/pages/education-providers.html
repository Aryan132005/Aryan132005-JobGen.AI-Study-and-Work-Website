Education Providers

Internship placement on behalf of Education Providers

Just graduating from university is no longer enough to get a job.

For universities and education providers, the expectations and demands from their students have intensified in terms of the work readiness and career education support they need to provide.

The employability of graduates is perhaps the best branding approach for universities and education providers, enhancing the value of their degree and reputation.

STUDY AND WORK helps students and graduates enter the industry by tapping into their skills to meet the needs of Australian businesses cost-effectively and efficiently. Our internship and graduate placement programs give employers an opportunity to utilise the skills, talent and insights of high achieving and career ready students and graduates at a reasonable financial cost.

We would like to partner with you to help your students gain quality internships and graduate positions by giving them immediate access to our huge network of quality host companies and employers. We have placed more than 8,000 interns and graduates in over 3,000 Australian and multinational companies since 2007.

We provide Professional Internship Program with a satisfaction guarantee, which means we make sure that the interns gain meaningful and relevant work experience in order to get the maximum benefit out of internship opportunity.

If the students want to receive academic credits against the internship, we can customise our Professional Internship Program to meet their specific academic requirements. We will ensure that they receive on-the-job training compatible with their course objectives. Many students want to do an internship for Work Integrated Learning, but they find it very difficult to source suitable internship placement themselves as securing an internship takes a lot of time and effort. They can seek our  help and focus on their studies instead of getting frustrated by cold-calling potential host companies. We can help them obtain the internship of their choice at none or a little cost.

We’d be extremely grateful if you could inform your students about our services. Please contact us if you have some strategies planned on how we can engage with them in a targeted manner.
